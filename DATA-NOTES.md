# Data notes

## Excluded on purpose
- Every row labeled SY 2021–2022, 2022–2023 or 2023–2024 (these are division template rows) and all other schools' rows.
- Teacher names and individual teacher scores. Only school-level and grade-level figures are shown.
- Filipino 3 CRLA row: it belongs to Tipolo ES, not Subangdaku.

5. **PHIL-IRI:** Subangdaku rows are blank.
6. **Nutrition total row:** the Term 2/Term 3 cells hold values (30, 3, 8, 2) that do not line up with any column header; Term 1 (60 wasted, 16 severely wasted) matches the per-grade rows, so only Term 1 is shown.
7. **Term 1 SAPM workbook (not used by these pages):** Grade 5 English and Science "Term Average" cells show values like 15.256 (a scaling error); Grade 5 PE & Health is tagged Grade 2; some Grade 3 term averages do not match their own components.
8. **BLIC and practice sheets:** the "Grade 2" practices sheet holds school-wide and Grade 3 entries; they are shown under the grade level written in each row.
